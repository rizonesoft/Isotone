## 3. The Content-Aware Engine: Fill, Scale, and Move

Content-aware fill, scale, and move are the features users name when they compare photo editors, and all three are classical algorithms (PatchMatch completion, seam carving) that run offline; generative fill is `D03 T19 §2` and never replaces them.
