# 09 Folium

> **Phases 45 and 46**

Folium, the suite's page layout and publishing app (Latin for a leaf of a book), planned on 2026-10-23 by the operator's request ("Now, for the sake of completeness, let's als oinclude planning for 3 more additions to the suite. Indesign clone, professional screenshot app, modeled after ShareX and PickPick, but not all the features. and a professional color picker and color studio."). It follows the first suite release (Phase 40) and is planned by the campaign itself, just ahead of building it: TODO-01 pre-screens the name, surveys Adobe InDesign, Affinity (its Publisher studio), QuarkXPress, and Scribus into the parity catalog [`../../docs/parity/folium-parity.md`](../../docs/parity/folium-parity.md), writes the layout map and section design, designs the accent, icon, and splash, and then authors Phase 45's files (the foundation and `folium-v0.1.0`) and Phase 46's (parity through `folium-v1.0.0`, which joins the `isotone-v1.1.0` suite bundle). Its scope is the operator's: an InDesign alternative; the operator set no fence, so, as for Stilus and Gesso, every InDesign feature is planned, with what Affinity, QuarkXPress, and Scribus do better.
