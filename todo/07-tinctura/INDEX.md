# 07 Tinctura

> **Phases 41 and 42**

Tinctura, the suite's color picker and color studio (Latin for a tint or dye), planned on 2026-10-23 by the operator's request ("Now, for the sake of completeness, let's als oinclude planning for 3 more additions to the suite. Indesign clone, professional screenshot app, modeled after ShareX and PickPick, but not all the features. and a professional color picker and color studio."). It follows the first suite release (Phase 40) and is planned by the campaign itself, just ahead of building it: TODO-01 pre-screens the name, surveys Microsoft PowerToys Color Picker, Just Color Picker, Adobe Color, and Coolors, with the color surfaces of Photoshop and Illustrator (the Color Picker dialog, the Color and Swatches panels, Color Themes) as the Adobe reference into the parity catalog [`../../docs/parity/tinctura-parity.md`](../../docs/parity/tinctura-parity.md), writes the layout map and section design, designs the accent, icon, and splash, and then authors Phase 41's files (the foundation and `tinctura-v0.1.0`) and Phase 42's (parity through `tinctura-v1.0.0`, which joins the `isotone-v1.1.0` suite bundle). Its scope is the operator's: "Picker plus full studio (Recommended)": a system-wide eyedropper with magnifier, every color space (HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV, Lab, LCH, OKLCH, ICC-managed CMYK), palettes and harmonies, gradients, palette extraction from images, WCAG and APCA contrast, color-blindness simulation, and exchange in ASE, ACO, GPL, CSS, and design tokens; licensed libraries such as Pantone are out.
