# 08 Capio

> **Phases 43 and 44**

Capio, the suite's screenshot and screen recording app (Latin for I capture), planned on 2026-10-23 by the operator's request ("Now, for the sake of completeness, let's als oinclude planning for 3 more additions to the suite. Indesign clone, professional screenshot app, modeled after ShareX and PickPick, but not all the features. and a professional color picker and color studio."). It follows the first suite release (Phase 40) and is planned by the campaign itself, just ahead of building it: TODO-01 pre-screens the name, surveys ShareX and PicPick (the operator's models), with the Windows Snipping Tool as the system baseline and Snagit, read from its documentation, as the quality bar into the parity catalog [`../../docs/parity/capio-parity.md`](../../docs/parity/capio-parity.md), writes the layout map and section design, designs the accent, icon, and splash, and then authors Phase 43's files (the foundation and `capio-v0.1.0`) and Phase 44's (parity through `capio-v1.0.0`, which joins the `isotone-v1.1.0` suite bundle). Its scope is the operator's: "Capture, annotate, record (Recommended)": every capture mode (region, window, full screen, scrolling, delayed, repeat last), a full annotation editor, OCR, pin to screen, history, and screen recording to MP4 and GIF; output to file, clipboard, and one configurable custom uploader. Out: ShareX's 80-plus upload destinations, automation workflows, and its grab bag of small tools.
