# TODO-01 -- Isotone.UI: the Shared WPF Library

> **Goal:** The suite's house style lives in one WPF library, `Isotone.UI`, that every app consumes: the icon catalog and `VectorIcon`, the splash and exception windows, the border glow, the theme resources generated from the design system's `docs/design/tokens.json` with live theme, Highlight, and density switching, the implicit control styles and the shared window chrome (title bar, document tabs, dock theme, status bar) every app's windows are drawn with, and the About and shortcuts dialogs once a second app needs them. No control or window exists twice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Current state (verified 2026-09-26):** There is no `src/Isotone.UI/`. Stilus and Pinxit each carry their own copy of the same UI pieces, grown from one origin: `VectorIcon.cs` (Stilus 145 lines, Pinxit 119), `IconService.cs` (68 and 69), `BorderGlowAnimator.cs` (209 and 209), `SplashWindow.xaml.cs` (121 and 115), and `ExceptionWindow.xaml.cs` (174 and 171). Each has its own `Resources/icons.json` (Stilus 7 lines, Pinxit 69), and the two differ. The palettes differ too: Stilus's `MainWindowView.xaml` defines pure greys (`Base` `#1A1A1A`), Pinxit's `Themes/Colors.xaml` defines Catppuccin Mocha (`Base` `#1E1E2E`) with an orange accent. Both apps load `IconService` through a static `Instance`.

## 9. The Visual Regression Harness and Goldens

The design contract (`standards/design-contract.md`, operator decisions 2026-09-27) defines pixel perfect as "Exact tokens + ±1 DIP geometry + approved goldens", with golden sign-off by the review panel only. Token and state tests (§5's `StyleContractTests` and state tests) prove resolved brushes, not pixels: a template can resolve every brush correctly and still draw a 3 px focus ring, a clipped glyph, or a 25 px row. This section builds the harness that renders every `Isotone.UI` control and surface offscreen per state, theme, Highlight, density, and scale, compares each render with its approved golden under `docs/captures/golden/`, and writes the side-by-side report against the design reference renders of `scripts/render-design-reference.py`. It runs after §3 (the harness needs the generated dictionaries) and before §5, so every control section from §5 on adds its scenarios here and earns its goldens in its own review; `D00 T01 §9` built the Design-line gate, design-lint, and the reference renderer it pairs with. The golden names, the tolerance, and the approval rule are the contract's section 3; this section implements them and restates none.

**Fidelity:** no surface of its own -- the harness is test infrastructure; the surfaces it renders are owned by §2 to §8 and by every later section that adds a scenario.
