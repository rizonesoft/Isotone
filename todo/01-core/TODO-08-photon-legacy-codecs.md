# TODO-08 -- Photon.Core Legacy Raster Codecs: Bitonal, Kodak, Icon, PICT, and Corel Formats

> **Goal:** `src/Photon.Core/Formats/` holds one managed, stream-based, fidelity-proven codec for each legacy raster format the parity catalogs name and more than one app reads: MacPaint, CALS Type 1, GEM IMG, XPM, and Scitex CT (§1); Kodak Photo CD and FlashPix (§2); Windows icons and cursors with the icon resources inside EXE, DLL, and ICL files (§3); Macintosh PICT as a drawing-operation model with a writer and a rasterizer (§4); and the undocumented Corel PHOTO-PAINT CPT, Corel Painter RIF, and Wavelet Compressed Bitmap WI, decoded from documented clean-room analysis of fixtures (§5). Nodus is the first consumer (`D02 T18 §5`, `§6`, `§8`), Imago the second (`D03 T17 §8` registers the XPM, ICO, and CUR codecs and `D03 T17 §9` the Scitex CT writer instead of building its own), and Lumen is the third (`D04 T13 §3` registers the XPM and icon codecs and `D04 T13 §8` the GEM IMG, Photo CD, FlashPix, PICT, and CPT codecs from here rather than a second copy). Codecs read from and write to a caller-supplied `Stream`; they never open, create, or replace a file, so every save goes through the consuming app's atomic writer. Opening never changes a file, and what a format cannot carry is reported, never silently dropped.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Current state (verified 2026-09-27):** There is no `src/Photon.Core/` project yet; `D01 T02 §1` creates it in Phase 2, and `D01 T03 §1` adds the `PixelBuffer` types these codecs decode into in Phase 9. No legacy raster codec exists anywhere in the suite: no MacPaint, Photo CD, FlashPix, Scitex CT, PICT, CALS, GEM, CPT, RIF, or WI reader or writer. Nodus names an ICO export format and options (`ExportFormat.Ico` and `class IcoExportOptions` in `src/Nodus/Bezier.Core/Services/ExportService.cs`) but has no ICO encoder or decoder; `D02 T14 §12` plans ICO decode through WIC in the WPF desktop project and CUR through that decoder path, which §3 replaces with a managed codec here. The Imago catalog plans XPM (IP-1807) and ICO (IP-1798) in `D03 T17 §8` and Scitex CT write (IP-1816) in `D03 T17 §9`; the Nodus catalog's legacy raster rows (NP-2391 to NP-2402) were backlog B-038 until the operator's 2026-09-27 decision planned them.

## Outcome

- One managed codec per format under `src/Photon.Core/Formats/` behind one small `IRasterCodec` contract (probe, decode to frames with metadata and a carry report, encode to a caller-supplied stream), and `grep` finds exactly one implementation of each.
- Every reader matches a named reference implementation pixel-exact for lossless formats and within a stated tolerance for Photo CD, FlashPix, and PICT; every writer's output decodes in that reference implementation to the source pixels.
- The ICO and CUR path Nodus built through WIC is replaced by the managed codec, and Imago and Lumen register the same codecs rather than building their own.
- CPT, RIF, and WI are either decoded against committed fixtures with their structure documented in `docs/dev/formats/`, or honestly rerouted with the analysis evidence; nothing ships partially.
- No codec opens, creates, or replaces a file, and every one decodes untrusted input without an unbounded allocation (a malformed fixture per format is refused with a named reason).

**Adjacency:** list=not-applicable (codecs keep no browsable records; the formats are listed in each app's open and export dialogs, D02 T18 §8 and D03 T17 §8); document=not-applicable (no printed output of its own); settings=not-applicable (codecs take option records from their callers; the persisted options are the consumers' settings in D02 T18 §8 and D03 T17 §8); reporting=applicable @ D01 T08 §1; notifications=not-applicable (progress and cancellation flow through the caller's IProgress and CancellationToken and are shown by the consumers' status strips); permissions=applicable @ D01 T08 §1; audit=applicable @ D01 T08 §1; exchange=applicable @ D01 T08 §1; reverse=not-applicable (a codec never changes a document or a file; undo and revert belong to the consumers)

**Adjacency rationale:** Reporting is the `CodecCarryReport` every decode and encode returns (what the format could not carry, per element), which the consumers show before writing. Permissions here means the refusal path: a truncated, oversized, or hostile file is refused by name with the offset and reason instead of crashing or allocating without bound, and a stream that cannot be read or written surfaces the I/O error unchanged. The audit trail is one Serilog Information line per decode and encode naming the format, dimensions, and the caller's path when it passes one. Exchange is the whole file: every codec reads a file somebody else authored, so each owes a format fidelity proof against a named reference implementation.

## 2. Kodak Photo CD and FlashPix Readers

Both are read-only, as in every competitor. Owns no catalog rows (the Nodus rows NP-2393 and NP-2398 sit on `D02 T18 §8`).
