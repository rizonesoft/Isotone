> **Goal:** Every file format Illustrator 30.8 and CorelDRAW 2026 still open, save, or export that the formats phase (`D02 T14`) did not cover opens in Nodus as editable objects or writes from Nodus, each proven against committed fixtures and a named golden: FreeHand, FXG, and SWF; Microsoft Publisher, Visio, and PowerPoint binaries; Corel DESIGNER and Micrografx Designer, Corel ArtShow, Presentations, R.A.V.E., and Picture Publisher; Macintosh PICT, OS/2 MET, GEM, Frame Vector Metafile, Lotus PIC, and NAPLPS; WordPerfect, Quattro Pro, Lotus 1-2-3, and WordStar; the legacy rasters (DCS, CPT, PCD, RIF, WI, CAL, ICO and EXE icons, FPX, MacPaint, Scitex CT, XPM) on the shared codecs of `D01 T08`; GIMP XCF with layers on the XCF core `D02 T18 §9` moves from Imago; and camera RAW through CorelDRAW's RAW Lab job on the suite RAW decoder and develop engine.
