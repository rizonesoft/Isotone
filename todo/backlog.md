# Photon -- Backlog

Ideas worth keeping that are not planned work. Nothing here is a section: an entry has no Implementation Order row, no phase, no checklist, and no owner, so no runner ever picks one up, `plan --check` never counts one, and nothing may defer work to one. The format spec, the admission test, and the budget are in [`README.md`](./README.md) under "The budget and the backlog"; the cap is `backlog_cap` in [`budget.json`](./budget.json).

**Entry grammar.** One line per entry: the id in brackets, the title, then ` -- key: value` fields. Required: `source` (one key token: a legacy key, a finding ID, or the `-> SOURCE:` key the section would carry), `added` (YYYY-MM-DD), `why deferred`, and `promote when`. Optional: `app`, `summary`, and `needs` (full `DNN TNN §N` refs to live sections, or other backlog ids). Ids are `B-NNN`, taken in order and never reused. `validate` refuses a malformed line, a duplicate id or source, an entry whose source a live section already carries, a dead or short section ref, and a count over the cap.

**Promotion** is `add-todo` with its admission test and a budget check: the promoting commit writes the real section (carrying this entry's source as its `-> SOURCE:` line) and deletes the entry. **Triage** is `groom-plan`: merge duplicates, drop stale entries with a one-line reason in the commit, and promote only with budget room.

**History.** B-001 to B-036 are the sections of the legacy-roadmap TODO files that nothing else in the plan needed, moved here on 2026-09-26 when the plan was bounded. Their full section drafts (Fidelity, Job, Treatment, Chrome, checklist, and checkpoint) are in git: `git show 1295471:todo/02-nodus/TODO-06-nodus-roadmap.md`, `git show 1295471:todo/03-imago/TODO-07-imago-roadmap.md`, and `git show 1295471:todo/04-lumen/TODO-03-lumen-roadmap.md`. Their old section numbers are retired and resolve to nothing.
