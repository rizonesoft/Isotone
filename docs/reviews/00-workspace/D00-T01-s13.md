## Self-review

1. Does it make sense? The three reported bugs are gone: the fingerprint takes `lstat` and never opens a link or a special file (under Linux the guard before this section raised `IsADirectoryError` on a link to a folder and blocked until killed on a link to a FIFO; the new one reads both at once), `acquire` keeps a live guard's phase on a re-point and refuses a first acquire without one, and `end` marks the guard ended in one atomic write before anything else, so every reader treats it as over even when the deletes never run. Around them: regular files open without following a link (`O_NOFOLLOW`/`O_NONBLOCK`, or `CreateFileW` with `FILE_FLAG_OPEN_REPARSE_POINT` on Windows) and are checked against `lstat`'s identity, the whole untracked walk runs in one daemon worker bounded by the deadline, every byte read counts against a total budget, and an incomplete walk is a named, recorded fail-open.
2. Is it logical? Only `end`'s exact mark (`ended_mark_ok`: a reason from `END_REASONS` and a canonical UTC timestamp) ends a guard, and the merger asks the same function; anything else present is a damaged guard. Pending cancellations are keyed by `(session_id, cron_id)`: confirming needs the session whose `CronList` proved the job gone, `reconcile` and the heartbeat act only on their own session's jobs, a record without a session is the operator's to clear (`--unowned yes`), and the operator's view names every owner.
3. Did it break anything else? The 127 cases before this section all pass among the 189; `merge_prs.py` reads the guard through `ended_mark_ok` (84 merge tests pass); `process-plan`'s heartbeat steps pass `--session` and settle their own job with `--if-pending yes` on every self-deleting path; the live campaign's own guard (written before the change, with no `ended` field) kept reading as `OWNER` throughout.

The same-family circuit breaker fired twice: after rounds 4, 9, and 10 on bounded I/O, the whole walk moved into one bounded worker; after rounds 1, 6, 7, 9, and 10 on cancellations, every record was keyed by session and job with one settle call. `source-defect` and `design-fidelity` lenses not owed beyond the independent pass's `source-defect` lens.
