## Lessons

- A gate that builds more than it reads pays for it twice: in time, and in every way the extra work can fail. `native-tidy.ps1` relinked 24 executables to refresh one header.
- A red that repeats on a re-run with a different victim is a race in our own steps, not a runner fault: look at what touched those files just before.
- Evidence added between a completion review and its reconciliation goes in an item's dated `Done YYYY-MM-DD:` note, never in a new paragraph of the section: `todo-graph.py stamp` reads anything else as a changed contract, and the record cannot be continued afterwards.
- A push while the previous push's `build` run is still going cancels that run: read the ship commit's build back before pushing review fixes, or accept that only the later commit has a build result.
- `review-prompt.py --base` takes a full 40-character SHA.
- Re-run the sanitizer lane on the final candidate and record it with the CI lines before the completion pass, not after it asks.
- With GoogleTest macros, `readability-function-cognitive-complexity` allows about six assertions per function: write a test as small named helper functions from the start.
- The analyzer cannot see a flag read through a reference captured by a seam; give the seam a `std::shared_ptr<const bool>`.
- A scripted rename over `.has_value()` also renamed a helper's own definition: check a mechanical edit's hits before building.
