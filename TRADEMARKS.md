- **Use the names to refer to the project.** Writing "Stilus", "Gesso", "Albumen", or "Isotone Graphics Suite" to talk about, review, teach, compare, or link to the official software is nominative use and needs no permission.

## What needs a different name and icon

- **Modified versions you distribute.** A fork, a patched build, or a repackaged installer that differs from the official build must use a different name and different icons, so nobody mistakes it for the official software or blames the project for changes it did not make. Change the product names, the app icons, the splash card, the installer's publisher, and the About dialog before you distribute.
- **Products and services.** Do not use the marks, or anything confusingly similar, as the name, logo, or domain of your own product, service, or company.

The GPL still applies in full to your modified code: this policy limits only the names and logos, never your rights to the code.
